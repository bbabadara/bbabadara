# Brand palette — Badara Ba

- Midnight navy: `#071426` — background and primary surfaces
- Champagne gold: `#D6A34A` — signature accent, separators and highlights
- Ivory: `#F2EFE8` — primary text and light surfaces
- Slate: `#8290A3` — secondary text and muted details

Design principles: restrained contrast, consistent spacing, editorial hierarchy, geometric linework, no portrait, no decorative neon effects.
